# Frog Soft-Mechanical Transformation Blueprint

## 1. Source Animal

Species:

Red-eyed tree frog.

Source identity:

- Compact amphibian body.
- Bright green coloration.
- Large protruding red eyes.
- Long slender limbs.
- Oversized orange adhesive toe pads.
- Blue and yellow side markings.
- Crouched resting pose.

---

## 2. Target Style

Soft-Mechanical Designer Toy.

Core style characteristics:

- Premium stylized 3D CGI.
- Rounded manufactured forms.
- Injection-molded polymer surfaces.
- Soft-touch dark structural components.
- Glossy optical eye elements.
- Restrained mechanical detailing.
- Clean product-design logic.
- Minimal surface noise.
- Studio product visualization.

---

## 3. Transformation Goal

Transform the red-eyed tree frog into an original soft-mechanical collectible creature while preserving the frog's unmistakable species identity.

The transformed design must still immediately read as a red-eyed tree frog.

---

## 4. Identity Preservation

### Tier A — Must Preserve

- Large protruding red eyes.
- Compact frog silhouette.
- Long slender limbs.
- Oversized adhesive toe pads.
- Bright green primary identity.
- Orange toe-pad accents.

### Tier B — Important

- Blue and yellow side coloration.
- Broad flattened head.
- Crouched pose.
- Minimal visible neck.

### Tier C — Flexible

- Fine skin texture.
- Minor wrinkles.
- Small biological surface details.

---

## 5. Body Transformation

Natural form:

Compact amphibian torso.

Soft-mechanical translation:

Convert the torso into a compact rounded polymer body shell with smooth continuous surfaces and broad radii.

Preserve:

- Compact body mass.
- Low posture.
- Broad upper body.

Avoid:

- Mammalian torso proportions.
- Humanoid chest.
- Overly segmented robot body.

---

## 6. Head Transformation

Natural form:

Broad flattened frog head with minimal neck.

Soft-mechanical translation:

Rebuild the head as a broad continuous polymer volume integrated smoothly into the torso.

Preserve:

- Wide head.
- Flat frontal profile.
- Minimal neck separation.
- Frog-specific mouth line.

Avoid:

- Human skull proportions.
- Long muzzle.
- Mammalian snout.

---

## 7. Eye Transformation

Natural form:

Large protruding red eyes.

Soft-mechanical translation:

Convert each eye into a large glossy optical lens module mounted within a rounded red accent housing.

Preserve:

- Strong protrusion.
- Wide spacing.
- Large scale.
- Red visual identity.
- Original orientation.

Materials:

- Glossy smoked-black optical center.
- Deep red polymer or translucent accent ring.
- Dark charcoal structural socket.

The eyes should remain the primary facial focal point.

---

## 8. Limb Transformation

Natural form:

Long thin flexible limbs.

Soft-mechanical translation:

Rebuild the limbs as elongated smooth polymer segments with restrained soft mechanical joints.

Preserve:

- Relative limb length.
- Slender proportions.
- Natural crouched orientation.

Materials:

- Green satin polymer main limb shell.
- Charcoal elastomer joint regions.

Avoid:

- Thick robot arms.
- Human elbows or knees.
- Heavy hydraulic joints.

---

## 9. Toe Pad Transformation

Natural form:

Large orange adhesive toe pads.

Soft-mechanical translation:

Convert the toe pads into oversized rounded soft-rubber contact modules.

Preserve:

- Large size.
- Rounded geometry.
- Orange color.
- Clear frog identity.

Material:

Dense molded rubber with soft satin finish.

These are Tier A identity features and should remain highly visible.

---

## 10. Surface Transformation

Natural surface:

Smooth amphibian skin.

Soft-mechanical translation:

Replace biological skin with clean satin injection-molded polymer.

Avoid:

- Skin pores.
- Wet biological gloss.
- Wrinkles.
- Organic micro-texture.

Use:

- Smooth controlled surfaces.
- Subtle molded transitions.
- Minimal seams.

---

## 11. Color Transformation

### Primary

Bright green body shell.

### Secondary

Deep charcoal structural regions.

### Accent

Red around optical eyes.

Orange toe pads.

### Supporting Color

Blue and yellow side panels inspired by natural markings.

The palette should remain recognizable but simplified into large controlled zones.

---

## 12. Marking Translation

Natural blue and yellow side markings should become:

- Small clean color-block panels.
- Shallow inset graphic regions.
- Minimal painted polymer zones.

Avoid literal biological striping or random decoration.

---

## 13. Mechanical Detail Strategy

Use restrained soft-mechanical detailing.

Allowed:

- Recessed seams.
- Small rounded-cone modules.
- Tiny vents.
- Controlled panel divisions.
- Soft joint housings.

Not allowed:

- Exposed wiring.
- Large gears.
- Military armor.
- Weapons.
- Heavy machinery.
- Cyberpunk detailing.

Mechanical features must remain secondary to animal identity.

---

## 14. Proportion Treatment

Preserve:

- Wide frog head.
- Compact body.
- Long limbs.
- Large eyes.
- Large toe pads.

Stylization:

- Slightly enlarge the eyes if needed.
- Slightly simplify limb thickness.
- Compact the body moderately.

Do not distort the frog into a generic toy creature.

---

## 15. Pose

Preserve the crouched resting pose.

Forelimbs should remain extended forward.

Hind limbs should remain folded in a recognizable frog posture.

The pose must support clear silhouette readability.

---

## 16. Expression

Preserve a calm, alert expression.

Avoid:

- Human smile.
- Angry eyebrows.
- Cartoon emotion.
- Human facial cues.

---

## 17. Material Mapping

Body:
Satin injection-molded polymer.

Structural joints:
Matte soft-touch charcoal elastomer.

Eyes:
Glossy smoked-black optical lenses.

Toe pads:
Dense orange molded rubber.

Color inserts:
Painted polymer.

---

## 18. Lighting

Use:

- Soft overhead-front studio key light.
- Gentle fill.
- Controlled broad highlights.
- Subtle rim light.
- Soft ambient occlusion.
- Clean contact shadow.

The lighting should reveal materials without creating dramatic cinematic effects.

---

## 19. Composition

Recommended default:

- Three-quarter front view.
- Full-body framing.
- Eye-level camera.
- Centered composition.
- Clean warm-gray or neutral gradient background.
- Product-shot presentation.

---

## 20. Negative Rules

Avoid:

- Wrong species.
- Extra limbs.
- Missing limbs.
- Mammalian anatomy.
- Humanoid anatomy.
- Generic robot.
- Military robot.
- Exposed wiring.
- Excessive panel lines.
- Sharp spikes.
- Photorealistic wet skin.
- Real skin pores.
- Random accessories.
- Busy background.
- Text.
- Logo.
- Watermark.

---

## 21. Transformation Summary

Animal DNA:

Red-eyed tree frog.

Style DNA:

Soft-mechanical designer toy.

Translation:

- Biological skin becomes satin polymer.
- Protruding eyes become optical lens modules.
- Toe pads become rubber contact modules.
- Long limbs become smooth articulated polymer forms.
- Natural colors become controlled industrial color zones.
- Biological detail becomes restrained molded geometry.

---

## 22. Final Blueprint

Preserve:

- Frog silhouette.
- Red protruding eyes.
- Long slender limbs.
- Orange toe pads.
- Bright green identity.
- Crouched pose.

Apply:

- Premium polymer materials.
- Restrained charcoal structural elements.
- Soft mechanical joints.
- Clean molded seams.
- Simplified color blocking.
- Product visualization lighting.

Avoid:

- Anatomy mutation.
- Generic robotics.
- Biological realism.
- Excessive mechanical detail.
- Style drift.

The final design should look like an original premium collectible soft-mechanical red-eyed tree frog while remaining immediately recognizable as the source species.
