# Frog Example — Animal DNA

## Species

Red-eyed tree frog.

---

## Silhouette

- Compact amphibian body.
- Broad head.
- Long slender limbs.
- Large protruding eyes.
- Oversized adhesive toe pads.
- Crouched resting posture.

---

## Body Proportions

- Head is wide relative to the torso.
- Torso is compact.
- Forelimbs are long and thin.
- Hind limbs are elongated and powerful.
- Toe pads are oversized relative to the fingers and toes.

---

## Head Structure

- Broad flattened head.
- Very large protruding eyes.
- Short muzzle.
- Wide mouth line.
- Minimal visible neck.

---

## Eyes

- Extremely large.
- Strongly protruding.
- Bright red iris.
- Dark pupil.
- Wide spacing.
- Major species-identification feature.

---

## Limbs

- Four limbs.
- Long and slender.
- Hind limbs longer than forelimbs.
- Flexible climbing anatomy.

---

## Feet

- Large adhesive toe pads.
- Rounded orange fingertips.
- Very important identity feature.

---

## Surface Type

Smooth amphibian skin.

---

## Natural Colors

### Dominant

Bright green.

### Secondary

Blue.

Yellow.

### Accents

Red eyes.

Orange feet.

### Neutral

Pale underside.

---

## Signature Features

### Tier A — Immutable

- Large protruding red eyes.
- Bright green body.
- Long slender limbs.
- Large orange adhesive toe pads.
- Compact frog silhouette.

### Tier B — Important

- Blue and yellow side coloration.
- Broad head.
- Crouched pose.

### Tier C — Flexible

- Fine skin texture.
- Minor wrinkles.
- Micro-surface details.

---

## Pose

Crouched on a surface with forelimbs extended forward.

---

## Expression

Calm and alert.

---

## Identity Summary

The animal must remain instantly recognizable as a red-eyed tree frog.

The most important characteristics are:

1. Protruding red eyes.
2. Bright green body.
3. Long slender limbs.
4. Large orange toe pads.
5. Compact crouched frog silhouette.
