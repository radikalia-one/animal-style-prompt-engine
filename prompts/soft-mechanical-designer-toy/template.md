# Soft-Mechanical Designer Toy — Universal Prompt Template

## Purpose

This template transforms any animal into an original premium soft-mechanical designer-toy creature while preserving its unmistakable species identity.

The template should be filled using:

- Animal DNA
- Style DNA
- Transformation Blueprint
- User instructions

Materials:
Use [PRIMARY MATERIAL] for major body surfaces, [SECONDARY MATERIAL] for structural or joint areas, [EYE MATERIAL] for the eyes, and [ACCENT MATERIAL] for signature anatomical features.

- Painted polymer inserts.

Keep all surfaces clean, premium, and factory-new.

Mechanical language:
Use restrained soft-mechanical detailing such as:

- Clean recessed seams.
- Rounded-cone modules.
- Minimal vents.
- Small inset housings.
- Soft mechanical joints.
- Hidden fastener logic.
- Controlled panel boundaries.
- Small modular inserts aligned with anatomical flow.

Mechanical details must remain secondary to the animal's silhouette.

Do not turn the animal into generic machinery.

Construction logic:
The character should feel manufacturable as a premium collectible toy using simplified modular construction, molded shells, hidden joints, clean panel intersections, and controlled part separation.

Detail density:
Keep large areas visually clean. Concentrate small mechanical detail around the face, joints, signature anatomical transitions, and selected focal regions. Avoid uniform greebling.
