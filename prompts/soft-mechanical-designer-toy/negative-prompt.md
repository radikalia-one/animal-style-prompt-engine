# Soft-Mechanical Designer Toy — Negative Prompt

## Purpose

This negative prompt defines the visual, anatomical, stylistic, and technical elements that should be avoided when generating animals in the soft-mechanical designer-toy style.

The goal is to preserve species identity, maintain clean industrial design, and prevent visual noise or unwanted style drift.

---

## Anatomy Errors

Avoid:

- Wrong species.
- Mutated anatomy.
- Extra limbs.
- Missing limbs.
- Fused limbs.
- Duplicated paws.
- Incorrect wing count.
- Incorrect tail structure.
- Deformed paws.
- Malformed claws.
- Incorrect hooves.
- Incorrect beak structure.
- Incorrect horn placement.
- Incorrect ear placement.
- Humanoid anatomy.
- Human hands.
- Human feet.
- Generic biped body unless the species naturally requires it.

---

## Facial Errors

Avoid:

- Distorted face.
- Crossed eyes.
- Misaligned eyes.
- Incorrect eye spacing.
- Deformed muzzle.
- Deformed beak.
- Broken jaw proportions.
- Human facial anatomy.
- Human lips.
- Generic cartoon smile.
- Excessive emotional exaggeration.
- Loss of species-specific facial identity.

---

## Biological Texture

Avoid literal biological micro-texture unless explicitly requested.

Avoid:

- Photorealistic fur strands.
- Individual hair fibers.
- Real feather fibers.
- Hyper-detailed scales.
- Biological pores.
- Wet organic skin texture.
- Veins.
- Wrinkles.
- Muscle striation.
- Realistic flesh.
- Biological gloss.

Natural surface information should normally be translated into clean molded design language.

---

## Mechanical Style Drift

Avoid:

- Generic robot.
- Military robot.
- Mecha armor.
- Cyberpunk armor.
- Steampunk machinery.
- Heavy industrial machinery.
- Exposed gears.
- Exposed motors.
- Exposed cables.
- Exposed wiring.
- Hydraulic pistons.
- Mechanical skeleton.
- Weapons.
- Cannons.
- Blades.
- Tactical equipment.
- Excessive armor plates.

The character must remain a soft-mechanical collectible creature, not a combat robot.

---

## Over-Detailing

Avoid:

- Excessive greebles.
- Random vents.
- Random screws.
- Random bolts.
- Dense panel lines.
- Uncontrolled segmentation.
- Uniform surface detail.
- Too many mechanical modules.
- Visual clutter.
- Noisy topology.
- Decorative details without construction logic.

Large clean surfaces must remain visible.

---

## Geometry Problems

Avoid:

- Sharp spikes.
- Unnecessary hard edges.
- Needle-like details.
- Extremely thin parts.
- Floating geometry.
- Broken intersections.
- Inconsistent bevels.
- Uneven panel thickness.
- Accidental gaps.
- Impossible joints.
- Non-manufacturable geometry.

---

## Material Problems

Avoid:

- Rust.
- Dirt.
- Heavy scratches.
- Corrosion.
- Damaged paint.
- Fabric.
- Leather.
- Raw wood.
- Concrete.
- Stone.
- Biological flesh.
- Excessive chrome.
- Mirror metal.
- Transparent body shells unless explicitly requested.

Preferred materials should remain clean, manufactured, and premium.

---

## Color Problems

Avoid:

- Random color placement.
- Excessive rainbow palettes.
- Oversaturated colors.
- Uncontrolled gradients.
- Camouflage.
- Neon cyberpunk color schemes.
- Too many accent colors.
- Loss of identity-critical animal colors.

Color must follow anatomy, construction, and Style DNA.

---

## Rendering Problems

Avoid:

- Flat lighting.
- Harsh shadows.
- Blown highlights.
- Excessive bloom.
- Heavy fog.
- Dramatic cinematic darkness.
- Extreme depth of field.
- Grain.
- Noise.
- Motion blur.
- Low resolution.
- Compression artifacts.
- Pixelation.
- Poor reflections.
- Incorrect perspective.
- Distorted lens effects.

The desired result is clean premium product visualization.

---

## Composition Problems

Avoid:

- Cropped signature features.
- Cut-off limbs.
- Extreme camera distortion.
- Distracting environments.
- Busy backgrounds.
- Action scenes that hide anatomy.
- Extreme foreshortening.
- Unreadable silhouette.

The animal should remain clearly readable.

---

## Style Exclusions

Avoid:

- Photoreal animal.
- Anime.
- Manga.
- Flat cel shading.
- Comic outlines.
- Low-poly style.
- Claymation.
- Plush toy.
- Fabric doll.
- Realistic taxidermy.
- Horror creature.
- Body horror.
- Zombie.
- Monster mutation.

Unless explicitly requested.

---

## Originality Protection

Avoid:

- Copying the exact reference character.
- Copying reference-specific accessories.
- Copying logos.
- Copying branding.
- Copying unique decorative arrangements.
- Copying proprietary character-specific features.
- Reproducing the exact reference pose when unnecessary.

Transfer only reusable visual language.
